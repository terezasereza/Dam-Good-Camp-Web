Dam Good Camp

Sun Up, Sun Down

Every day at camp splits into two phases — and they don't mix.

Daytime — Gather & Build

  • Collect materials scattered around camp
  • Repair broken structures before they fall apart
  • Build new facilities to grow the campsite
  • Spend the day's earnings on upgrades once night falls

Nighttime — Keep the Guests Happy

  • Animal guests check in for the evening
  • Every mood swing shows right on their face
  • No hammers allowed once the sun sets
  • End the night with a rating that decides tomorrow's budget
